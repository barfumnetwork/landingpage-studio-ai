# phoenix-volumetric-v1.glb

The volumetric phoenix. Generated by `scripts/phoenix_build_volumetric.py`, and it
passes the Phase 15 gate: `STATUS PASS`, 38/38 weighted checks.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Triangles | 87,088 (43,704 verts) |
| Dimensions | x 1.84 (wingspan) · y 1.24 (depth) · z 2.91 (height) |
| Materials / textures / animations | none — matte geometry only |
| Frame | Z up, facing -Y |
| Islands | 80 solid parts, one connected assembly, nothing floating |

## What is in it

- **Body**: one closed lofted shell from tail root to crown with elliptical
  cross-sections and a keeled chest. Torso local thickness is 0.66 of torso
  width, against 0.20 for the rejected relief emblem.
- **Head**: skull, S-curved neck, hooked beak as its own solid, five crest
  feathers.
- **Wings**: two arms with airfoil sections whose chord runs front to back and
  whose surfaces are pronated 33 degrees, so they read from a front camera
  without lying in the picture plane (plate normal 0.13 along depth).
- **Feathers**: 10 primaries, 9 secondaries and 8 coverts per wing, 9 tail
  plumes, 5 crest feathers. Every one is an individual solid with a rachis
  ridge, an asymmetric vane and a tapered tip. At mid-vane a primary measures
  0.045 thick on the shaft line against 0.003 at the vane edges.
- **Legs**: two tucked legs with three talons each.

## Pose

Rising, wings swept up into a V, tail streaming down and back. The pose is not
decorative. Two gate constraints pin it down:

- a full horizontal wingspan makes the front 3/4 projection too wide to ever
  reach 55-70% height in a 390x844 portrait frame;
- wings whose surfaces face the camera fail the wing-plane check, because a
  surface in the picture plane is what made the previous asset an emblem.

## Files

- `phoenix-volumetric-v1.glb` — the asset.
- `phoenix-volumetric-v1.landmarks.json` — head, hero feather and tail centres
  for the close-up framing, emitted by the generator.
- `phoenix-volumetric-v1.visual.json` — the visual sign-off, with caveats.

## Rebuild or re-check

```bash
blender --background --python scripts/phoenix_build_volumetric.py -- --out /tmp/phoenix-build

scripts/phoenix-pipeline/run.sh \
  --glb assets/phoenix/phoenix-volumetric-v1.glb \
  --label volumetric-v1 \
  --landmarks assets/phoenix/phoenix-volumetric-v1.landmarks.json \
  --visual assets/phoenix/phoenix-volumetric-v1.visual.json
```

Nothing here touches the chamber, the glass, the scroll camera or the lighting.
This is geometry only.
